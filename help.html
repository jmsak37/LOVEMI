Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot log in. What should I do?

Check your email verification status. If verification was not completed, LOVEMI will direct you back to the verification step.

I forgot my password.

Open Forgot Password from the login page and follow the account recovery process.

Why is my post not visible?

Submitted posts and media can require administrator review before public publication.

Why has my Premium access ended?

Premium access ends when its subscription reaches its configured expiry date or is otherwise restricted.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support ticket form on this page. Provide enough information for the support team to understand the issue.
