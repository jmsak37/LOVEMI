## Versioning Rules

Use:

MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

Example:

`1.0.0`

`1.0.1`

`1.1.0`

`2.0.0`

### PATCH

Bug fixes that do not change expected application behavior.

### MINOR

Backward-compatible features.

### MAJOR

Breaking changes or major architectural changes.
