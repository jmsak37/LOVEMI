Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to LOVEMI. This page explains how account, profile, communication and payment information is handled.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LOVEMI may collect information needed to create and operate an account, including username, full name, gender, email address, country, telephone number and account verification information.

Information may also be generated through your use of the platform, such as profile activity, connections, messages, notifications, payments and security logs.

2. Profile Information

Information displayed on a public profile is controlled by LOVEMI privacy settings and moderation rules.

Private account information such as ID information, authentication secrets, passwords and private security information is not intended for public display.

3. Photos, Posts and Videos

Premium members may submit permitted profile media, posts, photos and videos. Submitted media may require administrator moderation before becoming publicly available.

4. Communications

LOVEMI may process messages and connection information to provide the communication service, enforce platform rules, prevent abuse and investigate reports.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, security codes, identity documents or other sensitive information inside ordinary chats.

5. Payments

Premium payments are processed using the payment systems configured by LOVEMI. Payment records may include payment references, currency, amount, gateway and transaction status.

6. Security

LOVEMI uses authentication, access controls, database permissions, verification and security logging to protect the platform.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their passwords and verification codes private.

7. Your Choices

  • Update your profile information.
  • Adjust available privacy settings.
  • Manage connections and blocked users.
  • Delete your account where supported.
  • Contact LOVEMI support about privacy concerns.

8. Contact

Privacy concerns may be sent through the LOVEMI support system or by contacting eduassistasc@gmail.com.
