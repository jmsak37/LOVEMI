About the LOVEMI platform

Discover. Connect. Meet.

LOVEMI brings people discovery, profiles, connections, conversations, notifications and Premium services together in one organized experience. The platform is built to make every part of a member's journey clear, modern and easy to use.

Explore LOVEMI Learn More
Our purpose

A platform centered around people and connection

LOVEMI is designed to bring the important parts of a modern social experience together without making the interface complicated. Members can discover approved profiles, open secure profiles, create connections, communicate and manage their account from one place.

The experience is supported by server-side authentication, database-driven records, moderation controls and controlled access to account services.

Discover PeopleExplore approved members and use LOVEMI discovery to find people that may be relevant to you.
Build ConnectionsCreate and manage connections with other members through LOVEMI's relationship tools.
CommunicateContinue eligible conversations using LOVEMI messaging and notification tools.
Moderated ExperienceApprovals, reporting and administration help keep platform activity organized and controlled.
