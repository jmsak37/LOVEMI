About LOVEMI

LOVEMI is designed to make it easier for adults to discover people, create connections and communicate through an organized platform.

Our Purpose

LOVEMI brings discovery, profiles, connections, messaging, notifications and Premium services together in one platform.

The platform is built around database-driven accounts, controlled access, verification, moderation and administrator management.

Discover People Find profiles based on information available through LOVEMI discovery.
Build Connections Create connections and manage your relationship with other members.
Communicate Use the messaging tools available to eligible connected members.
Moderated Platform Reports, approvals and administration help keep platform activity organized.

LOVEMI at a Glance

The system is designed around a database-first architecture so important account and service states are determined from current server data.
