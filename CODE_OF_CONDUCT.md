# LOVEMI Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

LOVEMI aims to provide a respectful, safe, and professional environment
for users, developers, administrators, contributors, and support staff.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful.
- Communicate professionally.
- Avoid harassment.
- Avoid threats.
- Respect privacy.
- Respect intellectual property.
- Protect confidential information.
- Follow applicable laws and platform rules.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment.
- Threats.
- Abuse.
- Discrimination.
- Deliberate privacy violations.
- Credential theft.
- Unauthorized access.
- Malicious code insertion.
- Distribution of secrets.
- Deliberate disruption of LOVEMI services.

## Reporting

Concerns should be reported privately to the LOVEMI project owner or
appropriate administrator.

Do not publish private personal information in public issue reports.

## Enforcement

Administrators may restrict access, remove content, suspend accounts,
or take other appropriate administrative action when necessary.
