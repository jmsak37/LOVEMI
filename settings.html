Secure Account Deletion

LOVEMI will verify your identity before deleting your account.

1Request verification

Enter DELETE below. LOVEMI will send a verification email containing a secure link and six-digit code.

Your account is not deleted at this stage.
2Verify email

Open the verification link sent to your email, then enter the six-digit verification code.

Waiting for email link verification...
The code expires shortly for your protection.
3Google Authenticator 2FA

Enter the current six-digit code displayed by your Google Authenticator application.

Your normal Google Authenticator secret is never sent to the browser.
4Final confirmation

All security checks have passed. The next action permanently marks your LOVEMI account as deleted.

⚠️ Your profile will become unavailable, your active session will end, and your account will be marked deleted.
